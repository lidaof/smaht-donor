The scientific teams who initially analyze the donated tissues to do genome sequencing are called the “Genome Characterization Centers. Some of the smaller groups of scientists are called Tool and Technology Development teams. Together these teams will develop, test, and use innovative technology to figure out the most cutting-edge, efficient ways to find somatic variants. Then, they use their newly developed technology to analyze the data, look for patterns, and find somatic variants across different tissues and donors.
